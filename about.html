About Canvas

A new Guernsey studio, here to help small businesses look and sound their best.

Canvas Media started with a simple idea. Every small business deserves thoughtful, well-made branding and marketing, without it feeling out of reach or overcomplicated.

We keep that promise by pairing real design craft with the best modern tools, including AI. That means agency-quality branding, websites and social media at a price a growing business can actually justify.

We take on a small number of projects at a time, so each one gets proper attention.
